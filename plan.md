## What deliberately differs from the Maven twin (do not "resynchronise")
Everything else must be identical (`diff -r` excluding `build/`, `target/`, `node_modules/`, `.git/`, `.gradle/`): `src/main/java`, `src/main/webui`, `deploy/`, `gluonify.json`, all tests except `ConformityTest`.
- Build: `build.gradle.kts`, `settings.gradle.kts`, `gradle.properties` (versions, `gluonifySdkVersion`, `gluonify.sea.maven.url`) (the SDK comes from sea, without `mavenLocal()`), `gradlew*`, `gradle/wrapper/` (9.8.1) instead of `pom.xml`; `Dockerfile.build`, `.dockerignore`, `.gitignore`; `scripts/rename.py` (reads `build.gradle.kts` and `settings.gradle.kts`).
- `ConformityTest`: reads `build.gradle.kts` and `gradle.properties` instead of `pom.xml`.
- Four comments or messages that name the command: `DevAuthentication` (`./gradlew quarkusDev` instead of `mvn quarkus:dev`), `application.properties` and `App.test.js` (`./gradlew test` / `./gradlew build` instead of `mvn test` / `mvn package`).
- `README*.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `plan.md`: same texts, with the Gradle commands, the dependency snippet in Kotlin DSL and the Gradle property `gluonify.sea.maven.url` instead of the Maven property.
